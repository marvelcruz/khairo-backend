# Khairo Diet Clinic Standard Operating Procedures — Overview

This document outlines the core operational SOPs by role.

## Admin
- Manage users, roles, permissions, pricing, workflows and reports.
- Review Action Centre daily.
- Approve new tags, catalogue items and manual activation exceptions.

## Sales
- Work CRM leads from New Lead to Qualification.
- Qualify leads using the qualification form and recommendation.
- Book consultations and record outcomes.
- Send Paystack payment links from Payment Pending.
- Manage nurture and lost follow-up.

## Staff
- Support CRM data management and imports/exports.
- Maintain supplements inventory.
- Process orders and support onboarding tasks.
- Review Action Centre operational alerts.

## Coach
- Monitor client onboarding and Week 3 reviews.
- Complete Week 3 reviews and create support/escalation tasks.
- Follow up engagement and retention alerts.
- Manage client lifecycle pause/reactivate/completion.

## Doctor
- Only access Medical Review.
- Complete and sign assigned medical review cases.
- Record cleared / follow-up / not-cleared outcomes.
- Never modify CRM financial data.

## Client
- Use the portal for daily tracking, messages, payments, plans, and resources.
- Contact staff for support through the portal or WhatsApp.

## Communication
- Business-initiated WhatsApp marketing requires explicit opt-in.
- Use approved templates for WhatsApp broadcasts.
- Do not send free-form bulk messages.
- Respect client contact preferences and consent.

## Data Protection
- Never share client clinical data outside approved clinical channels.
- Respect role-based access controls.
- Do not create duplicate client records.
